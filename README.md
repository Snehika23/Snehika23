## 📂 Featured Projects

### 📡 [Customer Churn Prediction](https://github.com/Snehika23/Customer-churn-app)
> Predicting which telecom customers are likely to cancel their subscription

- Analysed 7,043 customer records across demographics, services, and billing data
- Handled class imbalance using **SMOTE** oversampling
- Trained a **Random Forest** model achieving ~80% test accuracy
- Built and deployed a fully interactive **Streamlit web app** — upload data, train model, predict churn in real time
- 🔗 **[Live App](https://customer-churn-app-a2hhngfkjwjeotbzdcwavr.streamlit.app/)**

---

### 💳 [Financial Fraud Detection](https://github.com/Snehika23/financial-fraud-detection-ml)
> Detecting fraudulent credit card transactions in a dataset with extreme class imbalance (0.17% fraud rate)

- Tested 3 models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost) × 3 resampling strategies
- Discovered that **Random Forest without resampling** outperformed all SMOTE-based models — F1: **0.8743**
- Precision of **94.12%** — 94 out of every 100 flagged transactions are genuinely fraudulent
- Deployed as a live **Streamlit app** for real-time transaction prediction
- 🔗 **[Live App](https://credit-fraud-app.streamlit.app/)**

---

### 🛒 [SuperStore Sales Dashboard](https://github.com/Snehika23/Superstore-sales-dashboard)
> End-to-end sales analytics and forecasting for a US retail business

- Analysed **5,901 transactions** across 2019–2020 — $1.57M total revenue across 49 states
- Identified that the West region leads with $522K in sales; South presents the biggest growth opportunity
- Uncovered that 42% of transactions are COD — a key operational insight for the business
- Built an interactive **Power BI dashboard** with KPI cards, regional breakdowns, and a **15-day sales forecast** using time series analysis
